# Quick start: booking with LPU Reserve

For students, faculty and staff. Five minutes to your first booking. Staff who run resources
should also read the [administrator and staff guide](guide-admin.md).

## 1. Sign in

Open LPU Reserve and sign in with your **VID** (registration or employee number) or username
and your password. Facility managers and administrators are also asked for a code from an
authenticator app ([guide-admin.md](guide-admin.md#signing-in-and-mfa)).

After five wrong attempts, sign-in pauses for 15 minutes.

## 2. Find what you need

On **Home**, answer *What do you need, and when?* in your own words, or use **Find**:

| Type | What you get |
|---|---|
| `lab for 40 tomorrow after 3 pm` | Computer labs seating 40 or more with free time tomorrow afternoon |
| `projector block 34` | Rooms with a projector in Block 34 |
| `basketball court free now` | Courts free right now |
| `semnar hall` | Seminar halls (spelling mistakes are fine) |

Each result shows a photo, capacity, location and how much of the day is free. The chips
under the search show how your words were understood; remove one to widen the search. Star a
resource to save it for later.

## 3. Pick a time

Open a resource to see its week. The legend explains every cell, and hovering or focusing a
cell says why it is or is not available:

- **Available:** you can book it.
- **Timetabled class:** the published timetable uses the room. Class time is never offered.
- **Booked**, **Pending approval**, **Maintenance**, **Blackout** (a holiday or exam period) or
  **Closed** (outside opening hours): not bookable now.
- **Your booking:** time you already hold.
- Greyed out: too soon (notice required), too far ahead, or not open to your role.

**Drag across the free time you want** (or tap a start and an end on a phone), then
**Review and book**. Add a title, the number of people and any accessories (such as an HDMI
cable), and book.

If something is refused, you are told why in a sentence, for example *"Equipment needs at
least 1 hour notice"*, with free alternatives nearby.

## 4. Your pass

A confirmed booking gives you a **pass** with a QR code (**Bookings → Open pass**).

- Some resources need approval first. The pass shows **Pending** and who is deciding;
  you are notified when it is approved or rejected.
- **Add to my calendar** downloads the booking as an `.ics` file. To have every booking appear
  in Google, Outlook or Apple Calendar automatically, copy your private feed link from
  **Me → Calendar sync**. Keep that link private: anyone with it can see your bookings.
- **Cancel booking** from the pass any time before the booking ends.

## 5. Check in, or lose the slot

When you arrive, check in in one of two ways:

- Open **Scan** in the app and point your camera at the **QR code on the door**, or
- Open your pass and press **Check in**.

Check-in opens shortly before the start (usually 15 minutes) and stays open for a grace
period after it (usually 15 minutes; the resource page shows the exact times). You get a
reminder before the start and a nudge when check-in opens.

**If you do not check in within the grace period, the booking is released for others and
counts as a no-show.** Repeated no-shows can pause your booking for some days. **Me → Booking
standing** shows your record and the rules. If you had a good reason, ask the resource's
custodian; they can forgive a no-show.

Finished early? Press **Check out early and free it up** on your pass so someone else can use
the rest of the time. Borrowed accessories are recorded as returned when you check out.

## 6. Something broken?

On a resource's page, choose **Something broken here? Report it**, say what is wrong and how
bad it is. The custodian is told straight away. A *critical* report from you alerts them
urgently; they check it and take the resource out of service if needed.

## Faculty and staff extras

- **Book for a class or group:** fill in *For a class or group* on the booking form. Either you
  or the students can check in.
- **Weekly booking:** **Bookings → Weekly booking** repeats a slot every week. Every date is
  previewed first; dates that clash (a class, a holiday, someone else's booking) are explained
  and skipped, and you book the rest in one go.

## Settings and your data

On **Me**:

- **Your limits:** how many hours or bookings you have left this week or month.
- **Language:** English, हिन्दी or ਪੰਜਾਬੀ. English covers the whole app. Hindi and Punjabi
  currently cover navigation, sign-in and the home screen; other screens stay in English.
- **Appearance:** light or dark (also the theme button in the rail).
- **Your data:** **Download my data** gives you everything LPU Reserve holds about you as a
  JSON file (DPDP Act 2023).

## Notifications

The **bell** at the top of every page opens your inbox. It collects booking confirmations, approval decisions, reminders, check-in nudges,
auto-releases and maintenance changes that affect you. Important ones are also emailed.

## Need help?

Ask the custodian named on the resource page (*Looked after by*), or the facilities office.
