# Customer demand survey (Google Form, about 4 minutes)

**Intro text for the form:** “We're students exploring whether canteens and messes could sell their safe,
unsold food at a lower price for pickup. This survey is anonymous unless you choose to leave contact
details. It takes about 4 minutes.”

1. **You are:** hosteller / day scholar / PG resident / working professional / other
2. **Where do you usually eat lunch and dinner?** (short answer)
3. **Typical spend per meal:** under ₹50 / ₹50–80 / ₹80–120 / over ₹120
4. **In the last 7 days, how many times did you skip or downgrade a meal because of cost or time?**
   0 / 1–2 / 3–5 / 6+
5. *Show this example card (an image or text):*
   “Veg thali ₹80 → **₹40** · Pickup 3:00–3:45 pm at Block 32 canteen · Eat by 4:15 pm · 6 left”
   **How likely would you be to reserve this?** 1 (not at all) to 5 (definitely)
6. **At what price would this feel like a bargain?** (₹)
7. **At what price would it be so cheap you'd doubt the quality?** (₹)
8. **At what price would it start to feel expensive?** (₹)
9. **Which pickup times could you realistically make?** (tick all) 11–1 / 1–3 / 3–5 / 7–9 pm / 9–11 pm
10. **Diet:** veg / vegan / eggetarian / non-veg
11. **Your biggest concern** (pick one): freshness / hygiene / taste / pickup hassle / “leftover” feeling /
    none / other
12. **Would you pay in advance by UPI to hold a meal?** yes / no / depends
13. **Would you join a WhatsApp group for a 2-week trial?** yes / no
14. *(Optional)* **Phone or email for the trial.** Add a checkbox: “I agree to be contacted about the
    FoodLoop trial. My details won't be shared or used for anything else.”

**Privacy:** don't ask for names unless needed, don't ask about health conditions or allergies by name, and
delete contact details after the pilot. That matches the data-minimisation spirit of India's DPDP Act,
2023.

**Reading the results:** stated interest is always higher than real behaviour. Treat “4–5 out of 5” answers
as an upper bound. The pilot's actual pickups are the real answer.
