# Canteen / mess / PG manager interview (20–30 min)

**Before you start:** ask permission to take notes. Explain it's a student project exploring surplus food,
that nothing will be published with their name without consent, and that there's nothing to buy.

**How to ask:** ask about *what already happened*, not what they *would* do. People are kind to your idea in
hypotheticals. Past behaviour is the truth (see *The Mom Test* by Rob Fitzpatrick). Don't pitch FoodLoop
until section D.

## A. Context
1. What kind of kitchen is this, roughly how many meals a day, and for whom?
2. Who decides how much to cook each day? Walk me through how yesterday's quantity was decided.
3. What makes demand go up or down? (Holidays, exams, weekends, weather, events, menu.)

## B. Surplus today
4. Think of the last five working days. On how many was food left over at the end of service?
5. Which items and meals are usually left over? Roughly how much on a normal day, and on a bad day?
   (Portions or kg.)
6. What happened to it? (Staff meal / given away / thrown away / animal feed / served the next day.) Ask
   gently, without judging.
7. Do you write any of this down today?
8. Between cooking and the end of service, how is the leftover food kept: on a hot counter, in a fridge,
   or at room temperature? For about how long?

## C. Worries and constraints
9. Have you ever tried selling leftovers cheaper, or giving them away? What happened?
10. What would worry you about selling surplus at a discount? (Regular customers waiting for discounts,
    brand, staff time, blame for food safety, permission from management.)
11. At what time of day could surplus realistically be handed over? Where in the canteen?
12. Who would post the food, and how comfortable are they with a smartphone?
13. How do customers pay today (UPI QR, cash, coupons, a mess card)?

## D. Compliance and decision-making
14. Is the kitchen FSSAI registered or licensed? Until when? Is there a Food Safety Supervisor?
15. Who would need to approve a trial? (Owner, contractor, college administration, warden?)

## E. The pilot
16. *(Now describe FoodLoop in two sentences.)* Would you try a free 2–3 week pilot that starts with one
    week of only logging leftovers (about 2 minutes a day)?
17. If it worked, what would it be worth to you: a commission per meal, a monthly fee, or only if it's
    free?
18. Is there anyone else I should talk to?

## After the interview (same day)
- Write up the **facts** (numbers, past events) separately from **opinions** (“sounds great!”).
- Note the three things that surprised you.
- Record: surplus frequency, typical quantity, storage method, current disposal, and willingness (yes / maybe / no).
