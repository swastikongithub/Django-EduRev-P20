**How the photos are used honestly**

- When a kitchen hasn't uploaded its own photo, a meal shows a matching stock photo labelled
  **“Representative image”**. It never pretends to be the actual food.
- Kitchens' own uploads always take priority over stock photos.

## Illustrations

The meal illustrations in `frontend/src/assets/meals/` were drawn for FoodLoop and are part of this
repository. They are the fallback whenever a photo is missing or fails to load.

## Statistics

Food-waste figures on the landing page come from the
[UNEP Food Waste Index Report 2024](https://www.unep.org/resources/publication/food-waste-index-report-2024).
FoodLoop doesn't publish any impact numbers of its own until they are measured in a pilot.
